# Protocol research credits

Mini Keyboard Studio contains an independent Swift/Objective-C implementation.
Its protocol behavior was cross-checked against these open-source projects:

- [Jl4cTuk/MINI-KeyBoard-V02.1.1](https://github.com/Jl4cTuk/MINI-KeyBoard-V02.1.1) — the compiled Windows configurator supplied by the user.
- [x0f5c3/ch57x-keyboard-tool](https://github.com/x0f5c3/ch57x-keyboard-tool) — MIT, © 2023 Mikhail Trishchenkov.
- [fleximus/macro_keyboard](https://github.com/fleximus/macro_keyboard) — MIT, © 2026 Felix Ehlers; documents a hardware-tested `1189:8890` output-only interface.
- [jvspier/macropad-rebind](https://github.com/jvspier/macropad-rebind) — MIT, © 2026 jvspier; documents the known firmware dialect differences and safety hazards.
- [EScripts-content/mini-3key-configurator](https://github.com/EScripts-content/mini-3key-configurator) — decompilation notes for the linked `HIDTester` executable and its 65-byte report framing.

No third-party binary or library is bundled in the app.
